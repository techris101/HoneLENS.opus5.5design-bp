HoneLENS BUILD GUIDE
Product definition, features, paywall, payment rail, and entitlement records
Prepared 4 October 2026


1. PROJECT DESCRIPTION

HoneLENS is a standalone browser extension for nursing students who need to check research sources, understand study results, practice nursing calculations, and organize their own evidence-based practice (EBP) work.

HoneLENS is part of the HoneNurse product family, but it must operate as a complete product by itself. Free study and research utilities should work without an account. A student who later uses other HoneNurse products should be able to move or connect their HoneLENS work through a portable, versioned HoneNurse export format. Do not require the HoneNurse website, KDP store, or another extension to use HoneLENS.

The product is AI-free by construction:

- No generative AI, LLM calls, hosted or on-device AI models, OCR/neural-network models, semantic/vector search, generated summaries, generated flashcards, auto-written assignments, AI scoring, or AI recommendations.
- Calculations use explicit formulas.
- Source checks use explicit rules and linked bibliographic metadata.
- Students select sources, answer appraisal prompts, interpret findings, and write their own conclusions.
- No document, highlight, note, or assignment text is uploaded for source checking or payment verification.
- Optional PubMed/Crossref lookups require the internet and must disclose exactly which identifier or search terms are sent. They are metadata requests, not AI requests.
- A no-AI product does not guarantee that every instructor permits every extension feature. Course rules may differ by instructor and assignment; students must follow their course policy.

The extension is local-first, not necessarily fully offline. Saved work, calculation tools, cards, and previously retrieved metadata should remain available offline. Fresh DOI/PMID and update/retraction lookups require a connection and must show when they were last checked.

Do not include OCR for scanned pages in the strict no-model version. Current Tesseract OCR includes LSTM/neural-network recognition, even when processing is local. Support selectable-text PDFs and keep image-only documents as attachments without claiming that their text was extracted. Do not use the machine-learned FSRS optimizer; use a fixed, documented Leitner-style review schedule.


2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Apply the existing HoneNurse design system directly. HoneLENS is a product surface within the same family, not a new independent visual brand. Reuse the approved logo system, typography, palette, component rules, spacing, and tone. Do not redesign the parent system or invent a new color palette for the extension.

Brand relationship:
- Parent brand: HoneNurse.
- Product name: Hone Lens / HoneLENS, following the established product naming pattern.
- Product line: “Keep trusted sources in view.”
- Parent slogan: “Sharpen your clinical judgment.”
- The product may use the idea “Show your sources. Keep your judgment yours.” as supporting copy; do not describe an activity log as proof of authorship.

Approved visual direction to apply:
- Mature, gentle, neat, restrained, readable, and calm.
- Manrope across the extension and the HoneNurse family. Use bold for headings and regular/medium for body and controls. Use tabular numerals for scores and timers.
- Evergreen Ink #143B3B for primary text, navigation, wordmark, and dark surfaces.
- Hone Edge #C9E85E for actions, progress, focus, and the logo accent. Do not use it as small body text where contrast is weak.
- Warm Paper #F5F6F0 for reading backgrounds.
- White #FFFFFF for cards and reversed text.
- Quiet Slate #667875 for secondary text.
- Keep the extension popup compact, but do not shrink the website into a narrow panel. Use an appropriately sized side panel or full-page workspace for multi-paper tables and document checks.
- Preserve accessible focus indicators, keyboard navigation, readable type scaling, and status meaning that does not rely on color alone.
- Avoid generic medical imagery and clutter. Explain why a check passed or failed in plain, measured language.
- Make page capture and promotion secondary to the nursing research workflow.


3. FEATURE INVENTORY AND PAYWALL

MARKETED FREE FEATURES

3.1 SOURCE CHECK

Purpose:
Help a student compare one source with the requirements of an assignment.

Behavior:
- The student enters the criteria: publication date window, source type, design requirement, population, or other explicit rubric fields.
- Retrieve bibliographic metadata by DOI, PMID, or supported article page when possible.
- Display available title, authors, publication date, journal, identifiers, publication type, and design signals.
- Each criterion returns “matches,” “does not match,” or “unknown,” with the rule and source of the result visible.
- Allow an optional evidence-framework selection and clinical question type. Only then may the extension show a provisional design-based category; label it “possible framework match—verify.” If no framework is selected, show design metadata only.
- Show any matched Crossref/Retraction Watch update or retraction record with a link and date.
- If no record is found, say “No matching record found in this lookup.” Never say “not retracted.”
- Do not automatically certify peer review, article quality, risk of bias, assignment acceptance, or clinical value.
- Show “What this check means” next to every result, including data source, last checked time, and limitations.

Paywall:
- Free: one-source checks and student-entered assignment criteria.
- Paid: batch checking across a source list and checking those records against a selected DOCX; see Source Audit.

3.2 DOSAGE TRAINER

Purpose:
Provide repeatable nursing mathematics practice without AI.

Problem types:
- Unit conversion.
- Tablet and liquid doses.
- IV rates and infusion times.
- Weight-based calculations.
- Pediatric calculation practice.
- Reconstitution.
- Other dosage templates only after nursing-educator review.

Behavior:
- Generate practice items from deterministic, bounded templates and validated values; never call an AI model.
- Show the formula, unit conversions, dimensional-analysis steps, answer, and rounding rule.
- Let students select the conventions taught by their program.
- Track practice locally by calculation type and user-selected errors.
- State that the tool is for education, not patient-specific advice or medication authorization.
- A nursing educator reviews templates, generated-value ranges, answer keys, units, and rounding behavior before release.

Paywall:
- Free: a generous, continuing practice bank and worked explanations.
- Paid: timed exams, advanced sets, and mistake analytics through Dosage Exam Simulator.

3.3 STATS CALCULATOR

Purpose:
Help students calculate and read common effect measures using explicit formulas.

Behavior:
- Accept student-entered event counts or rates and calculate risk ratio (RR), odds ratio (OR), absolute risk reduction/difference (ARR), relative risk reduction (RRR), number needed to treat (NNT), or number needed to harm (NNH) when applicable.
- Ask for the comparison groups, outcome direction, denominator, and follow-up period.
- Require baseline/control risk for absolute effects and NNT/NNH. Do not derive a context-free NNT from an OR alone.
- Permit the student to enter a reported relative estimate and confidence interval. Use fixed explanations specific to OR, RR, or hazard ratio (HR); identify whether the interval includes the null value of 1.
- State that an interval containing 1 does not prove “no effect,” and that crossing the null is not a measure of clinical importance.
- Show the formula, inputs, units, assumptions, and rounding.
- Do not parse a highlighted sentence and silently infer its variables or meaning.
- Have a nurse educator or statistician review definitions, formulas, edge cases, and wording before release.

Paywall:
- Core calculator is free. No paid AI interpretation tier.


MARKETED PAID FEATURES

3.4 EBP ASSIGNMENT BUILDER

Purpose:
Take a student from assignment instructions to a source-linked comparison and an editable submission packet.

Includes:
- Multiple course and assignment workspaces.
- Student-created rubric profiles and course-specific rules.
- PICOT fields the student completes.
- PubMed query builder that combines terms and synonyms selected by the student; show the exact query.
- Label the query “a starting search, not exhaustive.” Do not claim that it replaces a librarian or comprehensive database search.
- Reading sheet with original, design-specific prompts such as question, design, sample, measurement, findings, limitations, and relevance.
- Student-selected source quotations anchored to URL or PDF page. Answers remain student-authored.
- Multi-study evidence/synthesis matrix with editable columns and visible source links for each row or entry.
- Editable Submission Pack export to Word/CSV. The export contains student-entered notes, metadata, citations, criteria, and tables; it does not write conclusions or assignment prose.
- Preserve the distinction between quoted source text and the student’s own words.
- Do not auto-score study quality or generate an appraisal.

Free taste:
- One saved assignment.
- Up to three evidence rows.
- CSV export.

Paid:
- Multiple workspaces, saved rubric profiles, full matrix capacity, and Word/CSV Submission Packs.

License boundary:
- Use original wording for appraisal prompts. Do not reproduce CASP or JBI checklist text in a commercial product without the required commercial permission/license.

3.5 DOSAGE EXAM SIMULATOR

Purpose:
Extend the free trainer into a structured, timed test-preparation workflow.

Includes:
- Timed practice exams.
- Student-selected program pass target; do not present any one threshold as universal.
- Student-selected rounding and unit conventions.
- More advanced deterministic problem sets.
- Local mistake analytics by calculation type and unit/rounding issue.
- Worked review after an exam.
- No patient-specific dosing decisions, drug recommendations, or clinical “safe dose” outputs.
- Human educator review of every template and every answer path.

Free taste:
- One sample exam or limited sample mode.

Paid:
- Full exam sets, advanced practice, local result history, and mistake analytics.

3.6 SOURCE AUDIT

Purpose:
Check a group of sources and a student-selected DOCX before submission.

Source checks:
- DOI/PMID resolution and available bibliographic metadata.
- Duplicate DOI/PMID or likely duplicate records; title-based matches must be shown as candidates for confirmation.
- Date-window fit based on the student’s own selected cutoff.
- Crossref/Retraction Watch update and retraction record matches.
- Missing or conflicting source fields where a deterministic comparison is possible.

DOCX checks:
- Parse the selected file locally on the device.
- Match parsed in-text citations to reference-list entries and identify unmatched items.
- Check parsed APA 7 fields for visible omissions or formatting inconsistencies.
- Compare source dates with the assignment window.
- Scan against an abbreviation list provided by the student or course. Do not imply a single abbreviation rule fits all nursing programs.
- Put “Do Not Use” abbreviation checking here, not inside the dosage problem generator.

Output:
- Show each flagged passage/entry and why it was flagged.
- Show the matched metadata record and lookup time.
- Mark anything that could not be parsed or verified as “unknown” or “not checked.”
- Never rewrite the DOCX, generate missing text, or upload the file.
- Never claim that an absent Crossref match proves that no update/retraction exists.

Free taste:
- One audit preview with an issue count.

Paid:
- Full batch results, detailed local report, saved audit within the assignment workspace, and repeated checks.


QUIET FEATURES AND SOFT TIES

These are included and easy to find in the product, but do not need separate headline cards on the pricing page. “Quiet” does not mean concealing a data or permission practice.

3.7 PAGE CAPTURE
- Capture visible area, a selected region, or the full page by best-effort scrolling and stitching.
- Save the page URL, title, and capture time where available.
- Preserve the original page and source link beside the capture.
- Explain that protected browser pages and some lazy-loaded or complex scrolling pages cannot be captured reliably.

3.8 SOURCE CLIPPER AND SOURCE LOCATORS
- Save a user-selected excerpt with title, URL, time, and PDF page/section locator when available.
- A selected excerpt is a quotation until the student edits or comments on it.
- Return to the original source location when possible.

3.9 DOCUMENT INTAKE
- Read webpage text, selectable-text PDFs, and DOCX documents.
- Keep image-only documents as attachments. Do not OCR scans or handwriting in the strict no-model build.
- Report extraction failures instead of silently treating a partial extraction as a complete document.
- Run DOCX parsing locally for Source Audit.

3.10 HIGHLIGHTS, NOTES, AND BASIC READING SHEET
- Add highlights and margin notes.
- Store a source/page locator with each highlight.
- Distinguish quoted material from student-authored notes in the UI and exports.
- Provide basic static reading prompts at no cost; the paid Assignment Builder provides design-specific multi-study structure.

3.11 LOCAL LIBRARY, LITERAL SEARCH, AND FILTERS
- Organize sources by course, assignment, date, and tags.
- Search imported source text and student notes using literal text matching.
- Filter by source type, date, tag, course, or check status.
- Do not use semantic search, vector embeddings, model-based ranking, or inferred topic labels.

3.12 APA 7 CITATION MAKER
- Free utility for creating and editing citations from known source metadata.
- Format known fields and visibly flag missing information.
- Never guess an author, date, DOI, journal, title, or page range.
- Save citations with their source record.

3.13 SOURCE-LINKED FLASHCARDS
- The student writes each question and answer.
- Optionally begin a blank card from a selected source excerpt; do not auto-generate an answer.
- Store a source/page link with the card.
- Use a fixed, documented Leitner-style schedule. Do not use FSRS optimization or ML.
- Export to Anki-compatible text so cards remain portable.

3.14 LOCAL BACKUP AND HONEPACK PORTABILITY
- Provide a user-initiated backup and restore.
- Use a versioned, documented HoneNurse-compatible package containing the user’s selected data and source references.
- Avoid a proprietary lock-in format. Allow students to export their work even if a subscription ends.
- Do not include a source document in a backup unless the student selected that document for inclusion.

3.15 OPTIONAL ACTIVITY LOG
- Log only relevant actions taken inside HoneLENS, such as clipping a source, completing a checklist field, or editing a note.
- Let the student disable, clear, or export the log.
- Do not record keystrokes, other apps, or general browsing history.
- Describe it as a personal research trail. It is not proof of authorship, proof of AI non-use outside HoneLENS, or an academic-integrity certificate.

3.16 “WHAT THIS CHECK MEANS” EXPLANATIONS
- For every automated status, explain the rule, the data source, the lookup time, and known limitations.
- Examples: a date-window match only confirms a date; a metadata match does not certify peer review; no retraction match does not establish that none exists.

3.17 NETWORK DISCLOSURE AND LOCAL PRIVACY CONTROLS
- Clearly state that student notes, highlights, assignments, and DOCX files stay local.
- Before an optional lookup, identify the external service and the exact data being sent (for example, DOI, PMID, or search terms).
- Allow metadata results to be cached locally with retrieval date.
- Publish an accurate privacy policy and Chrome Web Store disclosures, including local processing and storage.
- Request only permissions needed for existing features and only when the user action requires them.

3.18 ACCESSIBILITY AND RECOVERY
- Keyboard operation and visible focus.
- Readable zoom and scalable layout.
- Status labels that do not rely on color alone.
- Clear save, import, export, cancellation, and recovery states.
- Preserve work if capture, parsing, lookup, or payment verification is interrupted.

3.19 HONE NURSE LINKS
- Offer optional, contextual links to related HoneNurse learning or practice material.
- Keep links dismissible and disable-able.
- Use a modest frequency cap; do not interrupt a capture, calculation, appraisal, or export.
- Do not infer sensitive interests from browsing or send reading topics to an ad/analytics service.
- Prefer a user-invoked “More from HoneNurse” link over a forced weekly popup.


4. PAYMENT RAIL

VERIFIED CHROME WEB STORE POSITION

Current Chrome Web Store documentation does not require Google’s payment system for extension subscriptions. Google’s current Developer Agreement says Google will not track or process paid-product transactions, authenticate paid users, maintain payment records, or handle taxes; the developer is responsible. The current payment policy also requires the developer to identify themselves as the seller and to publish clear product terms, including refund/return information.

Therefore:
- Publish HoneLENS as a free extension with clearly described optional premium features.
- Use an external payment provider, such as Lemon Squeezy, for checkout and subscriptions.
- The Chrome Web Store listing must clearly say which features are free and which require payment.
- Identify HoneNurse/the seller accurately; show subscription renewal, cancellation, refund, and support terms.
- Recheck the current Chrome Web Store Developer Agreement and policies before publishing, because policies can change.

Lemon Squeezy is a practical candidate because it supports hosted checkout, subscription billing, customer portal, license keys, and webhooks, and operates as a Merchant of Record for applicable transaction taxes. Its published base fee is 5% + $0.50 per transaction, with possible additional fees depending on payment type/location. Confirm merchant-country eligibility, verification, and payout support for the actual business before committing.

4.1 LEMON SQUEEZY PRODUCT CONFIGURATION

Create:
- A HoneLENS product in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard.
- A free extension listing separately in the Chrome Web Store.
- One premium subscription product with monthly and annual variants, or one-time variants if that is the chosen plan design.
- Enable license keys on paid variants if using Lemon’s license-key activation path.
- Set reasonable activation limits for devices and define the customer support/refund terms.
- Configure a customer portal so purchasers can update payment method, view invoices, or cancel without HoneNurse handling card data.

Keep all premium features in one understandable HoneLENS Plus entitlement unless there is a verified reason to create multiple paid tiers. The extension should not require a paid installation to use its advertised free features.

4.2 CHECKOUT FLOW

Recommended flow:
1. The student selects Upgrade in HoneLENS.
2. Open a Lemon Squeezy hosted checkout in a regular browser tab, or open a HoneNurse checkout webpage that embeds Lemon’s overlay.
3. The extension must not load Lemon.js or other remote executable JavaScript inside the extension package/popup/service worker. Manifest V3 requires extension logic to be included with the package; use the hosted checkout page outside the extension.
4. The checkout collects payment data. HoneLENS must never receive or store card numbers.
5. After payment, Lemon sends a receipt and, if enabled, a license key. The return page is a user-experience step only; do not grant paid access solely because the browser reached a success URL.
6. Activate the license or retrieve the server-side entitlement.
7. Refresh the paid feature state and show the plan name and renewal/access status.

Lemon Squeezy permits custom checkout data, which can include an internal HoneNurse user ID or one-time checkout reference. Do not use an untrusted client-supplied email or user ID as proof of payment. The webhook or license validation must confirm the purchase.

4.3 CODE COMPONENTS

Extension-side code:
- Upgrade screen/button that opens the hosted checkout URL.
- Plan/entitlement display for Free and HoneLENS Plus.
- Feature gate that enables paid-only UI after entitlement confirmation.
- License-key activation form if using license keys.
- Local cache for the last confirmed entitlement and its expiry/grace metadata.
- User action to refresh entitlement and deactivate a device/license instance.

Server-side code if using an account/database:
- POST /billing/checkout: authenticate the HoneNurse user, call Lemon Squeezy’s authenticated API, attach internal user ID as checkout custom data, return the hosted checkout URL.
- POST /webhooks/lemonsqueezy: accept Lemon’s webhook, verify its signature using the webhook secret, reject invalid signatures, and process each event idempotently.
- GET /api/entitlements: authenticate the extension account and return the current feature plan/status from the entitlement record.
- Optional POST /license/activate and POST /license/validate: proxy or manage device activation without placing the general Lemon API key in the extension.
- Customer portal route/button: open Lemon’s customer portal for billing self-service.

Secrets:
- Store the Lemon Squeezy API key and webhook signing secret in the server’s environment/secret store.
- Never put those secrets in extension JavaScript, a public repository, a website bundle, a mobile app, or a downloadable config file.
- Lemon’s separate License API uses the customer’s license key for activation/validation; do not confuse it with the authenticated store API. Store API keys must remain server-side.
- The extension package is inspectable. A local feature gate deters casual unlicensed use but is not strong DRM against a technically skilled user who edits the extension.

Webhook events to process:
- order_created and order_refunded.
- subscription_created and subscription_updated.
- subscription_cancelled, subscription_resumed, subscription_expired, subscription_paused, and subscription_unpaused where used.
- subscription_payment_success, subscription_payment_failed, subscription_payment_recovered, and subscription_payment_refunded.
- license_key_created and license_key_updated if license keys are enabled.

The webhook handler should:
- Verify the request signature against the configured webhook secret.
- Lemon payloads should not be assumed to provide a globally unique delivery ID. Construct a deduplication key from event name, resource type and resource ID, plus the resource update timestamp when available; store a canonical-payload hash as a fallback. Apply each state transition idempotently.
- Map Lemon product/variant IDs to known HoneLENS plan IDs.
- Update access after purchase, renewal, plan change, cancellation, expiry, refund, or payment failure according to the displayed terms.
- Preserve access through any explicitly configured paid-through period; do not revoke solely on a cancellation request if the subscription remains active through its period.
- Log processing result without storing payment card data or full sensitive payloads unnecessarily.


5. DATABASE AND WHO-PAID RECORDS

Lemon Squeezy already maintains customer, order, subscription, license-key, and payment records in its dashboard and API. That is the billing system of record. A separate database is not automatically required just to know whether a license key is valid.

The Lemon License API can activate, validate, and deactivate license keys. For subscriptions, a license key can follow the subscription lifecycle. The purchaser receives the key; HoneLENS stores the activation instance ID locally and validates it periodically. This can be the lowest-complexity design if:
- Free features work without an account.
- Research content stays on-device.
- Premium entitlement is a simple Free/Plus switch.
- No cross-device content sync or detailed user profile is required.

In that license-only design:
- Lemon’s dashboard/API is the record of purchasers and licenses.
- No application database is needed for a separate customer/plan list.
- Store license key and activation instance data in extension local storage, protect it from accidental display, and provide deactivate/reinstall handling.
- Define an offline behavior: keep the last verified Plus entitlement for a documented grace period, then request validation when online. Never erase student work when a license expires.

An application database is recommended if HoneLENS adds user accounts, multiple-device account access, automatic purchase-to-account matching, customer support recovery, more than one plan, shared entitlement across HoneNurse products, or optional cloud sync.

In that design:
- Lemon Squeezy remains the record of orders, payment status, taxes, and subscription source events.
- HoneNurse’s database stores only the minimum application identity and entitlement mirror needed to decide which features are unlocked.
- Do not store card numbers or payment credentials.
- Keep study documents/notes out of the billing database. Local project data remains in the extension unless the student explicitly enables a separate sync feature.

Suggested minimum relational records:

users
- id: internal UUID.
- email: only if account login/support requires it.
- created_at, updated_at.

plans
- id: internal plan name such as free or plus.
- lemon_product_id and lemon_variant_id.
- feature_entitlements: named features/limits associated with the plan.

subscriptions
- id: internal UUID.
- user_id.
- lemon_customer_id.
- lemon_order_id, where applicable.
- lemon_subscription_id.
- lemon_product_id and lemon_variant_id.
- status: active, on_trial, paused, past_due, cancelled-but-paid-through, expired, refunded, or other mapped state.
- current_period_end / ends_at.
- updated_at.

license_instances (only if using per-device keys)
- id: internal UUID.
- user_id or purchase reference.
- lemon_license_key_id; avoid storing plaintext key unless required.
- lemon_instance_id.
- device label, activation status, created_at, last_validated_at.

webhook_events
- deduplication_key built from event name, resource type/ID, and update timestamp where available; canonical payload hash as fallback.
- event_name.
- received_at, processed_at.
- processing_status and error summary.
- unique constraint on deduplication_key; keep processing idempotent because retries or reordered events may occur.
- store only the payload fields needed to reconcile entitlements; minimize retention of personal data.

entitlements
- user_id or installation_id.
- plan_id.
- feature keys/limits.
- status.
- source subscription/license reference.
- valid_until or last_confirmed_at.
- updated_at.

Database answer:
- Lemon Squeezy provides billing/customer/license records, so a separate database is optional for the first local-first version.
- Lemon Squeezy does not replace an application authentication and entitlement service if HoneNurse needs accounts, cross-device entitlements, app-specific plan rules, or integrations with other HoneNurse products.
- When that need exists, use a small managed relational database such as PostgreSQL and mirror only the minimum entitlement state from signed webhooks.
- Do not build a second payment ledger that attempts to replace Lemon Squeezy’s order/subscription records.
