Core Non-AI Guarantees:

  • Zero Generative AI: No LLM calls, hosted AI models, on-device neural networks, or vector embeddings.
  • Zero Manuscript Upload: Your .docx files and nursing papers are parsed strictly in memory. They never leave your device.
  • Deterministic Math: Dosage problems use verified medical formulas and validated numerical ranges.
  • Metadata-Only Requests: Optional lookups query public Crossref and PubMed APIs with only the raw DOI/PMID you provide.
